# Forage Job Simulations

## Overview

This repository contains four virtual job simulations completed through Forage, covering different professional functions within the insurance and data analytics industries.

The projects provided opportunities to apply theoretical knowledge to practical business scenarios, develop analytical solutions and communicate findings.

## Projects

| No. | Job Simulation | Focus Area |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Actuarial Analyst | Actuarial analysis and insurance risk |
| 2 | Claims Analyst | Claims assessment and analysis |
| 3 | Underwriter | Insurance risk evaluation and underwriting decisions |

## Skills Demonstrated

- Insurance and actuarial concepts
- Excel-based analysis
- Data cleaning and interpretation
- Analytical problem-solving
- Risk assessment
- Statistical analysis
- Technical reporting

## Purpose

This portfolio documents practical learning experiences and demonstrates the application of analytical and technical skills to simulated professional assignments.

Each project has a dedicated folder containing its relevant deliverables and documentation.
