# Marine Insurance Claim Analysis – AIG Claims Analyst Job Simulation (The Forage)

This repository documents the solution for the AIG Claims Analyst Virtual Experience Program on The Forage. The simulation required evaluating a marine/inland marine insurance claim involving temperature-sensitive goods and producing a professional analysis report in PowerPoint.

## Project Overview

- **Company / Program**: AIG Claims Analyst Job Simulation (The Forage)
- **Role Simulated**: Claims Analyst
- **Scenario**: Global Shipping Inc. submitted a claim for **$25,000** for alleged damage to temperature-sensitive goods during inland transit. A truck made an unexpected personal stop, temperature monitoring lapsed (not recorded during the stop), and the insured alleged the interruption caused damage. The claim is evaluated under the policy’s temperature control failure coverage.
- **Key Constraints** (as given in the exercise):
  - Continuous temperature monitoring was **not** maintained during the stop → Temperature Control Rider does **not** apply.
  - Required documentation (bill of lading, shipping logs, incident report, photographs) is assumed provided.

## Solution Summary

After reviewing the policy terms, claim documentation, and following a structured claims-handling process, the recommended settlement is:

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Claimed loss | $25,000 |
| Applicable sub-limit (Temperature Control Failure) | $10,000 |
| Policy deductible | $1,000 |
| **Final payable amount** | **$9,000** |

**Reasoning**:
- Temperature control failure during inland transit is a covered peril.
- Standard sub-limit of $10,000 applies (the Rider that would raise it to $15,000 requires continuous monitoring throughout transit, which was not maintained).
- No exclusions (willful misconduct, inherent vice, ordinary wear and tear, etc.) were triggered on the facts given.
- Settlement = applicable sub-limit minus deductible.

## Report Contents (PowerPoint)

The deliverable (`Marine_Insurance_Claim_Analysis_Report.pptx`) contains 10 slides covering:

1. Title / Claimant & Date
2. Summary of Claim Details
3–4. Steps Taken to Investigate the Claim (Initial review → Policy evaluation → Documentation review → Communication with claimant → Coordination with third-party experts → Internal review → Final recommendation)
5. Policy Analysis (Coverage limits, Sub-limits, Exclusions, Application to the claim)
6. Determination of Claim Amount (clear calculation)
7–9. Justification of Claim Amount (policy references + evidence reviewed + conclusion)
10. Conclusion and Recommendations (risk-management suggestions for the insured)

## Recommendations Made to the Insured (excerpt)

- Maintain continuous temperature monitoring devices throughout transit.
- Implement real-time automated temperature alerts.
- Strengthen driver compliance / prohibit unauthorized stops.
- Keep complete temperature logs and shipment records.
- Conduct periodic training for drivers and logistics staff on temperature-sensitive cargo.
- Periodically review insurance sub-limits against the value of goods carried.
