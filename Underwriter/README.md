# Lexington Healthcare Underwriting – AIG Underwriter Job Simulation (The Forage)

This repository documents the solution for the AIG Underwriter Virtual Experience Program on The Forage. The simulation required underwriting and quoting a professional liability and general liability submission for a medi-spa.

## Project Overview

- **Company / Program**: AIG Underwriter Job Simulation (The Forage) – Lexington Healthcare
- **Role Simulated**: Underwriter
- **Insured**: Diamond Medi Spa, LLC
- **Coverage Requested**:
  - Professional Liability: $1,000,000 / $3,000,000
  - General Liability: $1,000,000 / $3,000,000
  - Policy Form: Claims-Made
  - Retroactive Date: 2025-09-08
  - Policy Period: 2026-09-08 to 2027-09-09
  - Requested Deductible: $2,500 (current deductible is $5,000)

## Key Underwriting Decisions

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| Gross Revenue | $3,200,000 |
| Staff Schedule | 4 Aestheticians, 2 Medical Assistants, 1 Supervising Medical Director, 3 Registered Nurses |
| Claims History (past 5 years) | 0 claims |
| Automatic Declines Checked | None triggered (no PRP injections, no prior cancel/decline, procedures within licensure, background checks performed) |
| Deductible Selected | $2,500 |
| Loss Free Account Credit | 10% |
| Underwriter Modification | –20% (favorable account) |
| **Final Premium** | **$27,007.20** |

### Underwriting Summary
Diamond Medi Spa, LLC is a medi-spa generating $3.2 million in gross revenue with no reported claims in the past five years. The insured maintains a formal risk management program, conducts background checks on all staff, and demonstrates strong financial performance. Based on its favorable loss history, robust risk controls, and financial stability, a 20% underwriter credit has been applied. The requested $2,500 deductible is offered, resulting in a premium of $27,007.

## Process Followed

1. Reviewed the Submission File (coverage specifics, application, and financials).
2. Confirmed the application was complete and did not trigger any automatic declines under Lexington Healthcare underwriting guidelines.
3. Populated the Lexington Healthcare Rating Tool with:
   - Limits, coverage type, retro date, and deductible
   - Gross sales revenue and employee schedule
   - Claims history (0)
4. Applied credits:
   - Deductible credit
   - 10% Loss Free Account Credit
   - 20% Underwriter Modification (favorable qualitative review)
5. Prepared the formal quote using the Quote Template.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Application of underwriting guidelines and automatic decline criteria
- Risk assessment (financial strength, loss history, risk management practices)
- Rating tool usage and premium calculation
- Application of underwriting authority (credits/debits)
- Clear underwriting rationale and formal quote preparation
- Attention to detail with claims-made coverage, retro dates, and deductibles
